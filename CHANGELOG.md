## 🌟 Vision

Ascend is a personal mastery platform that helps users learn and master any skill through:

- AI-generated learning roadmaps
- Gamified progression
- Quests
- Flashcards
- Skill trees
- Progress tracking

Built on the belief that skills stick when they're tied to a real project — Ascend itself is the proving ground for that idea.
