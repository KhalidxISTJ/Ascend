Welcome to the About Ascend page!

Why is Ascend being developed?

The whole point of making Ascend was to have an application that has all tools I needed into one space that all work together so, I can go from point A to B in the most efficient route possible. I am someone who wants to become a polymath. As a result, I need an application that can help me in all aspect of my journey. I also want to help others so I am making this app for everyone to use for their own part of life.

What is Ascend?

Ascend right now is an application in development by me the developer. The goal of Ascend is to help you get from point A to point B in the most efficient route possible. Whether you want to become the best at a skill, learn a skill, or just want to be more productive, Ascend is the application for you. But it all come down to what you do at the end of the day. Ascend's job is supposed to make it as easy and clear as possible.

Features within Ascend

  • A way to track progress
  • A place to add raw data
  • Keep track of your day
  • For anyone and anything
Ascend is still in development so there are more features to come!
